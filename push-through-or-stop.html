KNOW WHEN TO PAUSE

PAUSE AND PAY ATTENTION.

Stop activity and seek appropriate care if something feels serious, is getting worse or changes how your body functions.

▲ Major swelling or bruising▲ Numbness or tingling▲ Inability to bear weight▲ Severe or worsening pain▲ Instability or giving way▲ Symptoms after a head impact

READ THE SIGNALS

SORENESS—OR A SIGN TO SLOW DOWN?

✓ More like expected soreness

  • Shows up after a new or harder workout
  • Improves with light movement and warm-up
  • Feels general across worked muscles
  • Gradually clears with rest and recovery

△ Worth pausing to investigate

  • Begins during a specific movement or incident
  • Changes your form or how you move
  • Feels sharp, localized or concentrated in a joint
  • Persists, worsens or repeatedly returns

These patterns provide context, not a diagnosis. If you are concerned, consult an appropriate healthcare professional.
