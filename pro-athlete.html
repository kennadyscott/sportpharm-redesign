Why a pharmacist belongs on your team

The same product can be two different things.

Clean sport

Don’t leave eligibility to a label.

  • “Natural” does not automatically mean low-risk
  • Hidden ingredients and cross-contamination are real and documented
  • Rules can depend on your sport, your governing body and the timing
  • Current requirements change — last season’s answer may not hold
  • What you take should be documented, not remembered
Explore Clean-Sport Support

SportPharm does not certify products as universally competition-safe, and no pharmacy can. What we do is help you evaluate the risk in a specific product, for your sport and governing body, and document the decision you made.
